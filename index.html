The Zaonex Difference

Why International Importers Partner with Us

We bridge Tanzania vast agricultural potential with exacting international buyers, upholding rigorous standards from harvest aggregation through final ocean transit.

Direct Cooperative Partnerships

We contract directly with verified farmer groups and cooperatives across Tanzania, eliminating unnecessary middlemen to deliver full traceability and fair prices back to farming families.

Strict Lab Quality Testing

Every shipment undergoes moisture calibration, impurity removal, sorting, and third party laboratory screening to certify strict compliance with destination country food safety standards.

Strategic Maritime Port Dispatch

Located with swift highway and rail connectivity to the Port of Dar es Salaam, enabling efficient container loading, prompt customs signoff, and direct ocean freight sailings.

Transparent Trade Contracts

We offer flexible Incoterms including FOB Dar es Salaam and CIF worldwide ports, backed by transparent letter of credit financing and rapid courier dispatch of original shipping documents.

Our Four Step Export Journey

Every shipment follows a transparent protocol from farm gate aggregation to secure container sealing and ocean vessel clearance.

01

Ethical Sourcing

Direct aggregation from verified farmer cooperatives with initial moisture checks and farm gate grading.

02

Precision Grading

Mechanical cleaning, destoning, optical grading, and moisture testing inside state of the art processing centers.

03

Inspection and Packing

Phytosanitary inspection, export document certification, and bagging in food grade jute, GrainPro, or ventilated boxes.

04

Port Loading and Transit

Direct container transport to the Port of Dar es Salaam, swift customs release, and reliable ocean transit to buyer ports.

Who We Are

Connecting Tanzania's Harvest to the World

Zaonex Agritrade Company Limited is a Tanzanian registered agricultural and export company, incorporated under the Companies Act, Cap. 212 and licensed by BRELA.

We bridge the gap between Tanzania's vast agricultural wealth and international markets. From smallholder farms across the Tanzanian regions to buyers in Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and beyond, we handle every step of the export journey with precision and care.

Our approach is rooted in fairness, working directly with farming cooperatives and ensuring that the value chain benefits those who grow the crops.

What We Do

Comprehensive Export Services

From farm gate to foreign port, we manage the entire agricultural export process so our clients receive quality produce with zero hassle.

01
Sourcing & Procurement

We work directly with smallholder farmers, cooperatives, and commercial farming estates across Tanzania's agricultural zones to procure the finest crops at competitive prices.

  • Direct farmer partnerships across 10+ regions
  • Cooperative and farmer group sourcing
  • Competitive pricing with transparent reporting
  • Seasonal availability planning
02
Processing & Grading

Before export, all produce goes through our quality control pipeline: cleaning, drying, sorting, grading, and packaging to meet international buyer specifications.

  • Mechanical cleaning and precision sorting
  • Moisture content management
  • Custom size and quality grading
  • Food grade packaging options
03
Certification & Documentation

We handle all export documentation and certification requirements, including government issued permits and internationally recognized quality certificates.

  • Phytosanitary certificates (TPHPA)
  • Certificate of Origin (TanTrade)
  • Organic & Fair Trade certifications
  • Commercial invoices & packing lists
04
Freight & Logistics

We coordinate sea freight from the Port of Dar es Salaam and Zanzibar, offering FCL (full container load) and LCL (less than container load) shipments to global ports.

  • Port of Dar es Salaam shipments
  • FCL and LCL container options
  • Refrigerated containers for perishables
  • Live shipment tracking
05
Customs Clearance

Our experienced customs team navigates Tanzanian export regulations, TRA requirements, and destination country import compliance to ensure smooth border crossings.

  • Tanzania customs (TRA) compliance
  • Export levy management
  • Destination import advisory
  • TANCIS system processing
06
Trade Advisory

We support international buyers and new importers with trade advisory services including market entry guidance, contract review, and quality assurance consultation.

  • Market entry & buyer matching
  • Contract & pricing consultation
  • Quality assurance advisory
  • Regulatory & compliance guidance
